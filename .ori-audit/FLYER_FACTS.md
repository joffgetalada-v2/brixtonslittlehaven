## Policies (Parent Handbook)
### Arrival & separation
- Parents/guardians say goodbye at the designated drop-off area; not allowed to stay
  inside play area or classroom (builds independence)
- Adjustment over a few sessions is normal; staff trained for smooth transitions
- Tips: short calm positive goodbyes, reassure return, comfort item helps, trust the team
### Drop-off & pick-up
- Release only to authorized adults on record; advance notice if someone else picks up;
  valid ID may be requested
- LATE pick-up: 15-min grace after dismissal, then ₱100 first 15 min + ₱50 per
  succeeding 15-min interval; extended supervision ₱250/hr when applicable
- EARLY pick-up: 15-min grace before regular dismissal, then the same ₱100/₱50
  schedule and ₱250/hr extended supervision (as printed on the flyer)
### Attendance & absence (effective June 8, 2026)
- Report absences at least 2 HOURS before class, else recorded unexcused (no
  exceptions, even with a valid reason given afterward)
- Illness: 3+ consecutive days absent requires a medical certificate on/before return;
  extra clearance may be required for contagious/prolonged illness
- Travel/events: inform at least 48 hours ahead; documents may be requested; travel
  does not qualify for pause/extension/refund
- Reserved-slot system: ALL absences count as consumed sessions. NO make-ups, NO
  refunds, NO credits, NO session extensions, NO transfers to another day, month,
  program, or child — ever. Applies regardless of illness, travel, weather, holidays,
  service disruptions, or personal circumstances
- Adjustment period (crying, clinginess) is NOT a valid reason to pause enrollment;
  sessions continue counting if a child voluntarily stops attending
### Health & safety
- Unwell children stay home; fever/vomiting/diarrhea/contagious infections need at
  least 24 hours recovery; medical certificate or fit-to-return clearance may be
  required after contagious or prolonged sickness
- Allergies must be disclosed; medication only if medically necessary + pre-approved
- Emergencies: first aid given, parents contacted immediately; drills practiced
- Snacks: small healthy snack + labeled water bottle; avoid nuts & common allergens
### Uniforms, ID & belongings
- Uniform required during program sessions: regular school uniform Mon-Wed, PE uniform
  Thu-Sat
- Label ALL belongings; pack extra clothes for spills/water play/messy activities
- School ID with unique QR code (Kriyo attendance, check-in/check-out); parents/
  authorized guardians scan at arrival & dismissal; ID worn at all times; IDs provided
  at the parent's expense (Pre-K founding promo includes a free ID & lanyard)
- Valuables/toys/gadgets/jewelry stay home; comfort items allowed during adjustment
  with teacher approval; disruptive items stored and returned at dismissal; center not
  responsible for lost/damaged personal belongings
### Communication & partnership
- Channels: Kriyo Parent App (real-time updates, messages, attendance, photos),
  official Facebook page, monthly progress reports, individual parent-teacher
  meetings, emergency announcements
- Concerns via proper channels + scheduled meetings; do not confront teachers/staff
  during active class hours
### Privacy, CCTV & child protection
- CCTV used for safety; parents may request staff-supervised viewing of their own
  child's footage (specify date/time); other children remain unseen; no recording,
  screenshots, or sharing; official copies only when necessary/emergencies

- Confidentiality: records, assessments, reports, photos, medical info, family details

- Zero tolerance: abuse, neglect, bullying, harassment, discrimination, unsafe behavior
